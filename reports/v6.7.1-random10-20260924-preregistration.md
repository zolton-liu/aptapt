## Scoring and success condition

The complete v6.6 rerun2 baseline is 2/10. The goal requires at least 6/10 on
this full fixed list, a gain of at least four passing tasks.

Every task receives exactly one new rollout. A pass requires Agent completion
and the unchanged public checker to exit successfully. Failed tasks stay in the
denominator. There is no failure-only rerun, result selection, manual artifact
repair, checker edit, or reuse of outputs from another experiment.

The interrupted v6.7 experiment is excluded entirely from this score. Its first
six rows (5 pass, 1 fail), partial DCC trajectory, and corrupted private input
snapshot remain preserved only as diagnostic evidence. In particular, those
five passes cannot be copied into v6.7.1.

This is a known-public-set local regression, not an official House or hidden-set
generalization estimate. Direct operator checker tests remain engineering tests,
not pass@1.
